Screen Time Impact

Learn how digital device usage affects your physical and mental health, and discover strategies for healthier technology habits.

Digital devices can affect your physical and mental health

Understanding Screen Time

Teens today spend an average of 6-9 hours per day on screens outside of schoolwork. While technology offers many benefits, excessive screen time can impact various aspects of your health and wellbeing.

What Counts as Screen Time?

Screen time includes using smartphones, tablets, computers, TVs, gaming consoles, and any other device with a digital display.

Physical & Mental Effects

  • Eye strain and digital eye fatigue
  • Disrupted sleep patterns
  • Reduced physical activity
  • Increased anxiety and stress
  • Potential social media dependence
  • Self-esteem and comparison issues

Creating Healthy Digital Habits

Balance is key when it comes to screen time. Try these strategies:

  • Use the 20-20-20 rule: Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds
  • Create tech-free zones and times in your home
  • Avoid screens at least one hour before bedtime

Quick Tips

  • Blue Light Filters

    Enable blue light filters on devices, especially at night

  • App Boundaries

    Set time limits for social media and gaming apps

  • Movement Breaks

    Take a 5-minute movement break for every hour of screen time
